Flights

For guests who are travelling from far away, we recommend flying into the Halifax Stanfield International Airport (YHZ). The camp is a 1.5-hour drive from the airport.

Rental cars

We highly recommend renting a car for your stay, available at the airport. The camp is rural and not walkable or accessible by public transit. If you are not able to rent a car and need transportation from the airport or elsewhere, please reach out to us and we'll do our best to find you a ride!
