Flights

For guests who are travelling from far away, we recommend flying into one of the following airports:

  • Ottawa International Airport (YOW), 2 hours away
  • Ogdensburg International Airport (OGS), 40 minutes away
  • Watertown International Airport (ART), 1.5 hours away
  • Montreal Pierre Elliott Trudeau International Airport (YUL), 2 hours away
  • Syracuse Hancock International Airport (SYR), 2.5 hours away

If you fly to a Canadian airport, you will need your passport to cross the border to get to Potsdam.

Rental cars

If you are flying, we highly recommend for your stay. Potsdam is rural and not accessible by public transit. If you are not able to rent a car and need transportation from the airport or elsewhere, please reach out to us and we'll do our best to find you a ride!

Driving

Potsdam is an accessible drive from these places:

  • Ottawa (2 hours)
  • Montreal (2 hours)
  • Toronto (4 hours)
  • Rochester (4 hours)
  • Long Island (7 hours)
  • Portland, ME (7 hours)
